How it's plotted: I took each still's five main colours (k-means on a small copy), weighted by how much of the frame they fill. Left to right is average lightness in OKLab, a colour space built around how eyes judge brightness. Bottom to top is how far the colours lean from blue toward orange. Both axes are stretched on a power scale so the crowded end gets room; the order is untouched. Thumbnails are nudged apart until none overlap, so a small dot marks where each one really measures. Bigger thumbnails are my picks, not a measurement.
